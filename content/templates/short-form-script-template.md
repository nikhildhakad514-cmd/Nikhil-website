## Packaging and quality check

- **Title (Required):** [Accurate, concise]
- **Caption:** [Context, source attribution, and accessible text; avoid unsupported claims]
- **Cover text:** [Optional; short and legible at phone size]
- **Visual direction:** [Framing, pace, light, type, supporting footage]
- **Orientation / aspect ratio (Required):** [e.g. vertical 9:16]
- **Asset paths:** [Video / graphics / audio / thumbnail locations]
- **Source credits / links:** [Where viewers can inspect sources]

- [ ] One central idea; no extra lesson crowded in.
- [ ] Hook creates a real question and the payoff answers it.
- [ ] Script sounds natural when spoken and runtime has been timed aloud.
- [ ] Example is specific, representative enough for its claim, and verified where necessary.
- [ ] Causal, psychological, and universal claims are appropriately qualified.
- [ ] Captions are accurate; text is readable on a phone and stays inside safe areas.
- [ ] Audio, visual clarity, rights, and accessibility are checked.
- [ ] Title and cover accurately reflect the final video.
- [ ] Final status, channel, URL, and publication date recorded.
