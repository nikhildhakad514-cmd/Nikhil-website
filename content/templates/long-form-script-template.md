## Recording and editing checklist

- [ ] Read aloud; remove stiff or unnatural phrasing.
- [ ] Time a spoken read and confirm runtime is plausible.
- [ ] Verify every factual claim, quote, name, date, and number against the source log.
- [ ] Preserve qualifications and uncertainty in narration and captions.
- [ ] Confirm story permissions, privacy, attribution, and asset rights.
- [ ] Mark required b-roll, graphics, on-screen citations, and aspect ratio.
- [ ] Record clean audio, room tone, and any alternate lines needed.
- [ ] Check captions, names, source credits, audio levels, pacing, and visual legibility.
- [ ] Confirm title and thumbnail accurately represent the finished video.
- [ ] Record final status, channel, publication URL, and date.
