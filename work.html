01 / Long-form script

Faking It: The Psychology of Confidence

A long-form script built around a reversal: confidence may follow action, rather than arrive before it.

The question

How do you challenge the familiar “just believe in yourself” message without replacing it with another empty slogan?

The approach

Start with the counterintuitive premise, question the assumption, bring in supporting ideas, then move toward a practical reframe.

Format

Long-form YouTube script. The original project brief targeted an 8–12 minute video.

My role

Scriptwriting and narrative structure, including the hook, transitions and takeaway.

02 / Short-form script

Why Discipline Beats Motivation

A short script about identity and consistency, designed to make one clear idea land in a limited amount of time.

The challenge

Discipline is a crowded topic. The task was to find a useful angle rather than repeat generic motivational advice.

The structure

Counterintuitive hook → familiar failure pattern → explanation → identity reframe → one takeaway.

Format

Short-form script. The original target length was 45–60 seconds.

My role

Scriptwriting and shaping the narrative around a single central idea.
