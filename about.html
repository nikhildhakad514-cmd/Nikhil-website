02 / The person

Interested in
how people think.

I’m a BBA student and creative freelancer in Gwalior, India. I’m interested in storytelling, communication, psychology, and the small choices that make an idea clearer or more memorable.

What connects it all

I like work that sits between thinking and making: understanding an idea, finding its shape, then giving it a form someone else can understand.

That has led me through scriptwriting, content planning, graphic design and creative projects for businesses and community initiatives. The formats change, but the underlying questions stay familiar: who is this for, what matters to them, and what is the clearest way to communicate it?

I’m less interested in looking like an expert than in getting better at the work.

This website is part portfolio, part notebook. It gives the work a home while leaving room for the thinking behind it, the experiments that do not quite work, and the ideas still taking shape.

Things I return to

Questions worth staying with.

These are interests, not claims of mastery.

Outside the deliverable

I read across psychology, philosophy, fiction and business. I’m also working on clearer communication, stronger storytelling and a more deliberate way of learning. The goal is to turn those inputs into original observations and practical work, not another stream of book summaries.

That process is ongoing. The notes section is where I intend to publish what survives some thought.
